Piano Port

Used for lighting keys in Light show mode, and for playing notes back to the piano in Learning mode.

Computer Port

RTP MIDI, USB-USB, or Bluetooth peer used with Synthesia and similar apps in Learning mode.

MIDI Mode

Light show: piano keys light the LEDs. Computer port is ignored.

In Learning mode, stop control_change messages (reverb, volume, etc.) from reaching the piano. Notes and guide lights still pass through.

MIDI events

Select text in the log to copy. Lines are tagged [piano] or [computer].
